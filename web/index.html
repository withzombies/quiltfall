THE HOUSE RULES

Get comfy. Get clever.

  1. Take turns placing a piece. Start with eight kittens each. Tap an empty square to place immediately. Moves are final.
  2. Make a little room. A placed piece nudges its neighbors one square away, including diagonally. Blocked pieces stay put; nudges never chain. Pieces falling off return to their owner’s pool.
  3. Grow your kittens. A line of three of your pieces graduates its kittens into cats. All three return to your pool. If there are several options, choose just one. With eight pieces on the bed, you can instead retrieve one, graduating it if it’s a kitten.
  4. Cats stand their ground. Kittens cannot push cats. Cats push both kinds.
  5. Claim the quilt. Finish your turn with three cats in a line, or all eight cats on the bed, to win. Only the player taking the turn can win.

Your game saves after every move. You can close your phone and pick it up later.
