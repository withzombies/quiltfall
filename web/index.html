THE HOUSE RULES

Get comfy. Get clever.

Two players, one cozy quilt. Here’s how to make yourself at home.

  1. 1Pick a comfy spot

    You each start with eight kittens. Pick an available kitten or cat, then tap an empty square. Take turns—moves are final.

  2. 2Give a gentle nudge

    Your new piece nudges its neighbors one square away—even diagonally, and even your own pieces!

    More about nudges

    If a destination is blocked, that piece stays put. Nudges happen together and never chain. Pieces that tumble off the quilt return to their owner’s pool, ready to play again.

  3. 3Help your kittens grow

    After the nudges, line up three of your pieces. The kittens grow into cats, and the whole group returns to your pool.

    More about growing cats

    Lines can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal, and can mix kittens and cats. A single eligible line graduates automatically. If there are several choices, choose just one highlighted group and tap Confirm selection.

    If all eight of your pieces are on the quilt and at least one is a kitten, you can instead tap a ringed kitten to retrieve and upgrade it, or tap an adult cat to return it to your pool. You can still choose an eligible group of three. Finish your choice before your partner’s turn.

  4. 4Meet the grown-ups

    Kittens can nudge kittens. Cats can nudge both kittens and other cats.

    A kitten beside a cat? That grown-up stays comfy.

  5. 5Win the quilt

    Finish your turn with three cats in a line, or all eight cats on the quilt. That cozy quilt is yours!

    Only the player taking the turn can win.

Your game saves after every move. Close your phone, put the kettle on, and pick it up later.
